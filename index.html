What the numbers mean

Clean streak = consecutive completed runs since the last Daily limit exceeded death: 12. Across all causes the streak is 3; longest clean stretch on record 8. 6 of 21 runs died; 4 of those were the daily cap. Total tokens burned: 35.4M. Treasury badge quotes usdc_total = 14.442 verbatim from vitals.json at build time (runway estimate is null, so none is printed). All figures recomputed at build; change the audit, rebuild, badges change.
